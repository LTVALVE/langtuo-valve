Product Range Rangkaian Produk Katup

Ball Valve Katup Bola

Floating & trunnion mounted ball valves, DN15–DN500, WCB / CF8 / CF8M, PN16–PN420 (Class 150–2500), fire-safe design, for water, oil, gas and chemical service. API 6D, GB/T 12237.

Gate Valve Katup Gerbang

Rising stem gate valves, DN15–DN600, PN16–PN160, resilient seated and metal seated, for water supply and wastewater treatment plants. GB/T 12234, API 600.

Globe Valve Katup Globe

Bellows seal and metal seal globe valves, DN15–DN300, PN16–PN160, zero-leakage stem design for steam, thermal oil and corrosive media. API 623, GB/T 12235.

Butterfly Valve Katup Kupu-kupu

Wafer and lug type butterfly valves, DN50–DN1200, PN10–PN25, EPDM/NBR/PTFE seats, ideal for water and HVAC systems. GB/T 12238, AWWA C504.

Check Valve Katup Satu Arah

Swing check and lift check valves, DN15–DN600, PN16–PN160, for backflow prevention in pipeline systems. GB/T 12236, API 594.

Control Valve Katup Kontrol

Electric and pneumatic actuated control valves with positioners for flow and pressure regulation in process automation.
