Importing Industrial Valves from China to Vietnam: HS Codes, RCEP Tariff & Customs Document Guide

📅 2026-10-13 👥 朗拓阀门技术团队 🔖 阀门选型 · 采购指南

越南正在成为东南亚增长最快的工业阀门进口市场之一。据贸易数据，越南2025年工业阀门进口额约 4.2 亿美元，其中来自中国的供应占比接近 50%。对中国阀门厂商来说，越南不仅是需求方，更因 RCEP 关税优惠与地缘优势成为东南亚采购的第一站。本文面向越南及东南亚的进口商、项目采购与贸易商，梳理阀门进口的 HS 编码、关税与清关文档全流程。

1. HS 编码与关税基础 HS Codes & Tariff Basics

工业阀门在海关协调制度（HS）中归入 第 8481 章（龙头、旋塞、阀门及类似装置）。常见细分如下：

HS 编码商品描述常见阀型
8481.30止回阀旋启式/升降式止回阀
8481.40减压阀（安全阀）安全阀、减压阀
8481.80其他阀门/类似装置球阀、闸阀、截止阀、蝶阀、调节阀

越南对进口阀门征收的关税取决于原产地：最惠国（MFN）税率一般为 20%–27%（8481.80 常见 25% 档），而持有 RCEP 原产地证（Form E）时，中国原产阀门可享受逐年降税，多数品类当前税率约 0%–15%，部分 8481.80 商品已降至 5% 左右。关税以外，越南还征收 10% 的增值税（VAT）。

2. 越南进口必需文档清单 Required Import Documents

正规越南清关至少需要以下文件，缺一不可：

  • 商业发票（Commercial Invoice）：中英文品名、HS 编码、数量、单价、总价、原产国；品名描述必须与报关单一致。
  • 装箱单（Packing List）：每箱数量、毛重/净重、箱号、唛头。
  • 提单或空运单（B/L or AWB）：收货人信息与发票一致。
  • 原产地证（Certificate of Origin, Form E）：享受 RCEP 优惠税率的关键文件，由中国海关或授权机构签发。
  • 材质证书（Material Certificate / MTC）：证明阀体/内件材质（WCB、CF8/CF8M 等）符合合同约定。
  • 水压测试报告（Hydrostatic Test Report）：证明出厂强度与密封试验合格（API 598 / GB/T 13927）。
  • 第三方检验证书（可选）：如 SGS、BV 装运前检验，大型项目常要求。

3. 清关流程与注意事项 Customs Clearance & Key Notes

越南海关使用 VNACCS 电子报关系统，进口商或其报关行在货物到港前即可申报。实务中需特别注意：

  • 品名与 HS 一致：报关品名、发票品名、箱单品名三者必须一致，避免归类争议导致查验延误。
  • 商标/品牌申报：阀门本体或包装上的品牌须如实申报；假冒或未授权商标可能被扣留，越南对侵权货物执法趋严。
  • 抽样检验：部分阀门批次会被要求送检或现场查验，建议保留 10%–20% 的到货时间余量。
  • 木箱熏蒸：木质包装须有 IPPC 熏蒸标识，否则可能被退运。

4. 采购端 Checklist Buyer-Side Checklist

  • 要求供应商提供 完整文件包：发票、装箱单、Form E、材质证书、测试报告——下单前先确认能否提供。
  • 确认 HS 归类建议：让供应商按 8481 系列给出归类参考，避免到港后才发现税率高于预期。
  • 约定 质量验收条款：出厂前水压测试录像、第三方验货、到港抽样方案写进合同。
  • 物流计划：深圳/广州口岸到胡志明、海防海运约 5–7 天，预留清关与陆运时间。

Southeast Asia is becoming one of the fastest-growing regions for industrial valve demand. According to 2025 trade data, total industrial valve imports in the region reached approximately USD 2.17 billion, with China supplying as much as 44.3% of that demand — making China the region's largest source country. For project procurement engineers, understanding the application boundaries of each valve type is the first step to controlling project cost and safety risk.

1. Five Basic Valve Types Compared

In industrial piping systems, ball valves, gate valves, butterfly valves, globe valves and check valves handle over 90% of isolation and throttling functions. The trade-offs depend on media, pressure, pipe size and actuation frequency.

Valve TypeCore AdvantageTypical Applications
Ball ValveFast operation, reliable sealing, low flow resistanceRefinery line isolation, water treatment main and branch shut-off
Gate ValveMinimal flow resistance when fully open, long service lifeLarge-bore main lines, fully-open/fully-closed service
Butterfly ValveCompact, lightweight, low torqueLarge-diameter water treatment, cooling water circulation
Globe ValveLow seat wear, ideal for frequent throttlingInstrument and sampling lines, flow regulation
Check ValveAutomatic backflow prevention, protects pumps and compressorsPump discharge, compressor lines, drainage systems

2. Refinery Project Selection Logic

2.1 Pressure and Temperature Ratings

Refinery units involve high-temperature, high-pressure media. Valve pressure classes should start from Class 150, with most critical points in the Class 300–900 range. For steam and hot-oil service above 200°C, pay special attention to the temperature limits of both body and seat materials.

2.2 Governing Standards

Refinery and petrochemical projects across Southeast Asia commonly require compliance with API 6D, API 600, API 602 or ASME B16.34. Buyers should require suppliers to ship with material test certificates (MTC) and third-party inspection reports to avoid acceptance delays on site due to missing documents.

2.3 Body Materials

  • WCB carbon steel: best value for general oil, steam and gas lines
  • CF8 (304 stainless steel): general chemical and mildly corrosive media
  • CF8M (316 stainless steel): chloride-bearing seawater, acidic and strongly corrosive service

3. Water Treatment Project Selection Logic

Inlet and outlet lines of municipal and industrial water treatment plants are often above DN300. Here butterfly valves become the preferred choice for large-bore sections thanks to their compact size, light weight and low actuation torque; gate valves are commonly used on trunk lines that do not require frequent operation, to keep flow resistance low.

Because Southeast Asia is hot, humid and coastal with salt spray, discs and seats are recommended as EPDM/NBR lined with CF8/CF8M stainless steel stems, balancing corrosion resistance and sealing life. Moisture-proof and marine packing should also be included in procurement requirements.

4. Vendor Procurement Checklist

  • Does the supplier hold third-party quality management system certification?
  • Do products meet the project-specified API / ANSI / GB standards?
  • Can the supplier provide complete material test certificates (MTC) and pressure test reports?
  • Are sample pressure and seat leakage tests supported before batch orders?
  • After FOB Shenzhen dispatch, does the ocean lead time fit the project schedule? (Ocean freight is arranged by the buyer or their forwarder.)
  • Are installation guidance, spare parts lists and bilingual English/Chinese technical documents provided?

1. HS Codes & Tariff Basics

Industrial valves fall under Chapter 8481 of the Harmonized System. Common subheadings: 8481.30 check valves, 8481.40 safety/relief valves, 8481.80 other valves (ball, gate, globe, butterfly, control valves). Vietnam's MFN duty on most valve categories is 20%–27%; with a valid RCEP Certificate of Origin (Form E), Chinese-origin valves enjoy staged tariff cuts, currently around 0%–15% for most items. An additional 10% VAT applies.

2. Required Import Documents

  • Commercial Invoice — description, HS code, quantity, unit price, total value and origin must match the customs declaration.
  • Packing List — per-carton quantities, gross/net weight, case numbers and shipping marks.
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — consignee details consistent with the invoice.
  • Certificate of Origin (Form E) — essential for RCEP preferential duty.
  • Material Certificate (MTC) — proves body/trim materials (WCB, CF8/CF8M, etc.) meet the contract.
  • Hydrostatic Test Report — shell and seat tests per API 598 / GB/T 13927.
  • Third-party Inspection Certificate (SGS/BV, optional) — often required for large projects.

3. Clearance Process & Key Notes

  • Vietnam customs uses the VNACCS e-declaration system; declarations can be filed before the vessel arrives.
  • Keep description, HS code and brand name consistent across invoice, packing list and declaration to avoid classification disputes.
  • Declare trademarks truthfully — unauthorized brands risk detention as Vietnam steps up IP enforcement.
  • Wooden crates must carry IPPC fumigation marks or the shipment may be returned.

4. Buyer-Side Checklist

  • Confirm the supplier can provide the full document package (invoice, packing list, Form E, MTC, test report) before ordering.
  • Ask for an HS classification reference so the landed duty is not a surprise.
  • Put quality acceptance terms in the contract: hydrostatic test video, third-party inspection, arrival sampling.
  • Sea freight Shenzhen/Guangzhou to Ho Chi Minh or Hai Phong takes about 5–7 days; allow extra time for clearance.
